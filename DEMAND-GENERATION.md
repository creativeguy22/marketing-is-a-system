# Demand Generation

## Building Pipeline, Not Just Leads

Demand generation should answer one question:

> How do we consistently create qualified opportunities?

The framework:

```text
ICP
 ↓
TARGETING
 ↓
MESSAGE
 ↓
OFFER
 ↓
CAMPAIGN
 ↓
ENGAGEMENT
 ↓
MEETING
 ↓
OPPORTUNITY
 ↓
PIPELINE
```

---

# Start With the Math

Before launching campaigns, understand the revenue target.

For example:

```text
Revenue Target
      ↓
Required Pipeline
      ↓
Required Opportunities
      ↓
Required Meetings
      ↓
Required Responses
      ↓
Required Reach
```

This allows marketing to work backward from the business goal.

---

# Campaign Strategy

I like campaigns built around a specific problem.

Instead of:

> "Learn more about our platform."

Build around:

> "How security teams can eliminate a specific operational problem."

A strong campaign has:

* Clear audience
* Clear pain
* Strong point of view
* Compelling offer
* Multiple channels
* Sales alignment
* Measurement

---

# Paid Acquisition

Paid media is useful when:

* The ICP is identifiable
* The message is strong
* The offer is compelling
* Conversion can be measured

I monitor:

* Spend
* CTR
* CPC
* Conversion
* Cost per lead
* Cost per meeting
* Opportunity rate
* Pipeline
* CAC
* Revenue

The metric I ultimately care about is not the cheapest lead.

It's the most efficient **qualified pipeline.**

---

# Content

I don't believe companies need more content.

They need better content.

Content should answer:

**What does our buyer need to know before they buy?**

Content can:

* Create awareness
* Educate
* Build authority
* Support sales
* Address objections
* Accelerate evaluation
* Improve conversion

---

# Conversion

Traffic without conversion isn't demand generation.

I audit:

* Landing pages
* Calls to action
* Forms
* Offers
* Messaging
* Social proof
* Page speed
* Follow-up
* Mobile experience

---

# Measurement

My preferred hierarchy:

### Activity

What did we do?

### Engagement

Did anyone care?

### Conversion

Did they take action?

### Pipeline

Did it create an opportunity?

### Revenue

Did it create business value?

---

# Optimization

I don't launch and walk away.

I test:

* Audience
* Message
* Creative
* Offer
* Landing page
* CTA
* Follow-up
* Sales motion

The objective is continuous improvement.

---

# Demand Generation Principle

The goal isn't:

> Generate more leads.

The goal is:

> **Generate more of the right opportunities at an economically sustainable cost.**
